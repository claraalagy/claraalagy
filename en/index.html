Hi!

I am a PhD candidate. My research explores social justice in the socio-ecological transition. As part of my PhD, I study relationships between grassroots citizen groups and local governments in the circular economy through a gender lens.

I like climbing, music and cycling. I like building things, fixing things and making up stories. I like sharing and learning. I like laughing with my friends and making strangers laugh in the street.

I am interested in the meaning of practices and in how they shape imaginaries and possibilities. I question prescribed and negotiable forms of citizenship in contemporary Western societies.
