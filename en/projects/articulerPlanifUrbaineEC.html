Research project

Connecting urban planning and the circular economy:

Diagnostic tools, urban planning criteria and specialized public policy instruments.

Equipping municipalities to integrate circular economy principles into land-use planning practices

About

This project examines how urban planning can foster the development of the circular economy (CE) in Canada through public policy instruments adapted to local governments. It uses urban metabolism as a framework for developing planning criteria that optimize resource use and minimize waste. The study compares the range of instruments deployed in six major cities and assesses their capacity to integrate grassroots citizen initiatives. The findings are expected to identify new barriers and planning criteria and to support the development of practical tools for municipalities. The project also aims to strengthen Canadian expertise in CE and share innovations with local communities and the academic sector.
