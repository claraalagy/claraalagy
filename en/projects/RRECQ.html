RRECQ Equity, Diversity and Inclusion Committee

Quebec Circular Economy Research Network

Fostering innovation in circular economy research by promoting diversity and better research practices

About

The Equity, Diversity and Inclusion (EDI) Committee of the Quebec Circular Economy Research Network (RRECQ) works to better integrate equity, diversity and inclusion into the Network’s activities and, more broadly, into circular economy research. It is particularly interested in the conditions for a socially just circular transition that takes existing inequalities and risks of exclusion seriously.

Its work addresses both research practices and the operation of the Network itself. The committee seeks to foster greater diversity of disciplines, profiles and perspectives among members, integrate EDI considerations into governance, and encourage more inclusive research practices. It also examines the place given to marginalized people and groups in the production of circular economy knowledge, as well as their participation in defining research issues and benefiting from research outcomes.

Contribution

I first joined the RRECQ EDI Committee as a research assistant before taking on its coordination. In this role, I organized and facilitated committee meetings while contributing to the design and delivery of its various projects.

My main contribution was producing the report Best Practices for Improving EDI in Teamwork, for which I conducted the full documentary review (academic literature, grey literature and online resources) and wrote the report. The work identified practices that can foster more inclusive research collaborations, with particular attention to diversity in disciplines, methodological approaches and academic trajectories. It resulted in concrete recommendations for research networks and their members.

I also proposed and designed a survey to develop a portrait of EDI within RRECQ and better understand members’ experiences, perceptions and needs. I developed the questionnaire and programmed the survey before handing the project over to my successor for deployment.

Finally, I inventoried EDI resources and training tools to create an accessible toolkit for Network members, designed to support the integration of EDI into research and collaborative practices.
