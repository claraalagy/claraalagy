Research project

Social economy embedding circular economy:

Theory, practice and potential. An international partnership.

Co-creating knowledge on the circular economy and cooperatives through an international network

About

The transition to a circular economy requires contributions from many different actors. Social economy organizations, including cooperatives, are particularly important because they combine economic activity with social goals and democratic, locally rooted decision-making. The Social Economy Embedding Circular Economy partnership brought together researchers and practitioners from Europe and the Americas to develop practical tools, generate new knowledge and strengthen cross-sector collaboration.

Results

One of the main outcomes is the first international collection of open-access resources devoted to the circular economy, available through the PortailCoop digital library. The collection makes research accessible and usable, showcases examples of circular cooperatives, and provides a practical toolkit. The toolkit includes workshop guides built around a circular business model canvas as well as fact sheets on business models and policies designed to support social economy organizations and policy makers.
