Climate Equity Lab

Equity tools for climate change adaptation

Developing a toolbox of equity-oriented adaptation resources to support authentic dialogue on sensitive issues and provide tools that can act as levers for change.

About

Climate change adaptation measures are crucial today and will become even more important in the future. Many groups and researchers are concerned that proposed solutions may exacerbate inequalities, an issue that remains insufficiently studied and addressed. Scientific literature and field experience show that adaptation measures can create or deepen inequalities in several ways: through unequal distribution of impacts (distributive justice), unequal opportunities, capacities and outcomes of participation (procedural justice), and incomplete or uneven recognition of diverse forms of knowledge and experience and of intersecting forms of oppression and discrimination. Participatory approaches, along with stories and testimonies grounded in the knowledge and experiences of marginalized people, are increasingly recognized as having strong potential to place equity more centrally within adaptation processes. The Climate Equity Lab brings together diverse knowledge and experience to support the co-production of an Equity in Climate Change Adaptation Toolbox, designed to raise awareness and support practitioners in designing and implementing just adaptation solutions.

Contribution

I helped build a database designed to identify and make visible existing tools for equitable climate change adaptation. The database is intentionally non-exhaustive and allows users to become familiar with existing resources and equip themselves for their own processes. It is continuously evolving as new tools emerge in Quebec. The resources listed come from the knowledge of members of the Climate Equity Lab advisory committee, partners, the project team and web research.
