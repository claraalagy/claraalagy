Type Sports involvement

Role Member
Location Grenoble, France

About

Engineering studies at Grenoble INP are punctuated by two large annual events. The first, the Olympiades, brings together all Grenoble INP schools for a weekend in which some 5,000 students compete in sporting, musical and artistic events. The second, the CPPiades, is specifically for students in preparatory programs. Preparatory programs from across France gather in one city to compete in sporting and artistic events.

Involvement

From the moment I arrived at Grenoble INP, I took part in these activities. For three years, I played on the rugby and soccer teams and also performed with the cheerleading team. This involved weekly training alongside the demanding workload of the preparatory program. I enjoyed it so much that I ended my ‘career’ by dislocating my elbow during a tackle that was a little too intense for me.
