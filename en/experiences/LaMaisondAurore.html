La Maison d’Aurore

Homework help and academic support workshops for young people with learning difficulties in the Plateau-Mont-Royal.

Type Volunteering
Period 2022-2023
Role Volunteer
Location Quebec, Canada

About

La Maison d’Aurore is a multigenerational neighbourhood organization with several areas of activity in the Plateau-Mont-Royal. It provides opportunities for residents from a range of socio-economic backgrounds to volunteer and become involved in their community. One of its programs, the Château d’Aurore, is specifically designed for young people in the neighbourhood who experience difficulties at school. It offers homework-help workshops for elementary-school students referred by Plateau-Mont-Royal schools and the CLSC. Volunteers of all ages are paired with a student whom they support twice a week.

My involvement

For a full school year, I supported Lou, an elementary-school student with whom I built a strong relationship of trust. Lou had difficulty concentrating and needed plenty of opportunities to move. Together, we experimented with playful and dynamic ways of learning through games. Lou eventually became a Scrabble fan—and even beat me several times!
