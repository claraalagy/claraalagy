About

The Quebec Circular Economy Research Network (RRECQ) is an interinstitutional and interdisciplinary research network working to accelerate the circular transition, particularly in Quebec. It now brings together more than 200 researchers and experts from a range of backgrounds. Its mission is to structure, fund and advance interdisciplinary research that addresses concrete challenges in resource management, public policy and business model transformation. By connecting academia with socio-economic actors, RRECQ plays a strategic role in building a more resource-efficient, circular and resilient Quebec economy.

Involvement in the network

For the past four years, I have been actively involved in the Quebec Circular Economy Research Network (RRECQ).

From 2022 to 2024, I coordinated the Network’s EDI Committee, helping structure its work and develop tools to foster more inclusive research practices. In particular, I wrote a report on best practices for collaborative teamwork (see the Publications section).

I also contribute to RRECQ’s scientific direction as a member of the steering committee for TPS D3, which focuses on work, employment and skills in the circular economy, as well as the expert committees for TPS B3 and E2.

I was involved for more than six months in a Network-led podcast project on the circular economy, which unfortunately could not be completed due to lack of funding.

Finally, I take part each year in the Network’s conferences and scientific activities, including several presentations at Acfas, participation in BistroBrain, and membership in the RRECQ delegations to CIEC 2024 and 2026 in France.
