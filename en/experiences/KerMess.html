About

Ker-Mess is a Montréal arts collective combining circus arts, performance, installations and music. Founded by Arthur van Havre in 2023 to take part in Quebec’s regional burn, the group now includes around forty artists, musicians, resourceful builders and performers. Beyond our desire to contribute to Montréal’s underground cultural scene, what connects us is the pleasure of making dreams and ideas emerge together from almost nothing. Reuse and material recovery are core priorities for the collective. It is an extraordinary space for freedom and creativity: as Arthur often says, we don’t say ‘no’ at Ker-Mess.

Selected projects

Pandémonium 2024 :
Pandémonium is the annual fundraising event organized by L’ESPACEMAKER, a community makerspace in Hochelaga. Each year, around 1,200 people explore the space after it has been transformed for one night, raising more than $50,000. Artists and collectives take over different areas to build temporary worlds. In 2024, Ker-Mess transformed the metal workshop into a punk circus. Inspired by the industrial setting, we built a darker, rougher circus using materials that had originally been destined for the ecocentre.

Pandémonium 2025 :
For the 2025 edition of Pandémonium, Ker-Mess was given the venue’s largest room and asked to create a world around the theme of fire and water. We chose a pirate-circus concept and built a shipwreck from wooden pallets large enough to host two full stages. The entire room was also decorated to match the theme.
