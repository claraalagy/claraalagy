About

The Mechanics Calendar is a project from Montréal’s cycling community, particularly its community bike workshops. After not being published since 2019, the project was revived under the Bécyks Unis Montréal (BUMBUM) coalition. The Mechanics Calendar is a nude calendar that celebrates the diversity and creativity of the cycling community. Sales help fund the coalition and support the launch of new community bike workshops across Greater Montréal. Producing the calendar brought together a large number of volunteers from different workshops, and a launch party was organized for volunteers from across the network.
