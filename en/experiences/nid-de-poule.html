Nid-de-poule

Community bicycle repair workshop.

Type Community involvement
Period 2025–present
Role Co-coordination and facilitation
Location INRS-UCS, Montréal

About

Located at the UCS building since summer 2023, the Nid-de-poule bicycle workshop is a collective space for UQAM and INRS students that promotes active mobility, autonomy and community involvement. Located on the first basement level, the workshop provides tools and equipment so people can maintain and repair their own bicycles while learning in an accessible and collaborative environment. The workshop relies largely on student volunteers organized through several committees (equipment, outreach, communications and budget). Mechanical training, collective activities and group rides help build skills, confidence and safer use of urban space in an inclusive and welcoming setting.

My role

At Nid-de-poule, I contribute to coordinating, facilitating, developing and sustaining the workshop. I am involved in fundraising and in developing new projects, including an introductory cycling program designed to make cycling and mechanical skills more accessible to everyone. Nid-de-poule is, for me, a space where people can gain autonomy, share their skills and learn from one another. It is a concrete way to support active mobility, reuse and access to technical knowledge.
